Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check the progress of my complaint?

You can check the status at any time by entering your Ticket ID into the search bar at the top of this page, or by logging into your Student Dashboard.

What is the typical resolution turnaround time?

Urgent safety and electrical issues are prioritized within 4 to 12 hours. General IT and hostel complaints are normally resolved within 24 to 48 hours.

Can I reply to administrative comments?

Yes! When logged in, click "View Details" on any ticket to access the live conversation thread and post follow-ups directly to the assigned department.
